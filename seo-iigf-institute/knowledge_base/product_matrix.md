# Programme & Topic Matrix — When to Mention What (IIGF Institute)

Primary reference for `ppp-knowledge-sme-researcher` (what topic to flag) and `seo-article-writer-id` (placing client mentions in context).

> **Per-month keywords:** keywords are defined per month in `monthly_runs/<YYYY-MM>/keywords.md` and fully replace prior sets. The rows below are a **reference library for recurring/known topics**. For a **new keyword not listed**, derive its mapping from `keywords.md` (`topic_focus` / `client_anchor`) + research. The **universal rules below apply to ALL keywords.** The cousin/cross-link map is rebuilt per month from that month's keyword set.

> **Framing for this client:** IIGF Institute is the **knowledge-management unit and think-tank of PT Penjaminan Infrastruktur Indonesia (Persero) / PT PII** — not a standalone legal entity, not a university, and **not the owner of the CP3P scheme**. It **menyelenggarakan pelatihan · meneliti · menerbitkan · memfasilitasi jejaring**. APMG International owns and awards CP3P; IIGF Institute delivers the training as an Accredited Training Organization (ATO). Max **2–3** client self-mentions per article.

> **Content goal (locked 2026-08-11): educate first, then a soft CTA to the relevant programme.** Teach the KPBU/PPP topic thoroughly and vendor-neutrally; the programme earns the close. This protects think-tank credibility while still generating leads. Never open or middle-load an article with a training pitch.

## Universal rules (apply to every keyword — listed or not)

- ❌ Never imply IIGF Institute owns, awards, issues, or accredits the CP3P credential. APMG International does.
- ❌ Never present IIGF Institute as a university, a degree-granting body, or an independent legal entity.
- ❌ Never name an individual (hard blacklist — see `company_profile.md`).
- ❌ Never publish a fee or schedule without an "indikatif" label and its reference year.
- ❌ Never state a hard membership number for UNIID/JPII — use the range with its year.
- ❌ Never claim a JIPM indexing status the researcher has not verified.
- ❌ Never call IIGF Institute or PT PII an **anggota Kantor Bersama KPBU**. The official Kemenkeu list is 7 kementerian/lembaga and includes neither. Use "berkolaborasi dalam ekosistem Kantor Bersama KPBU" (`company_profile.md` Rule 5).
- ❌ Never list **MIF** among the CP3P developers — legacy 2016 wording, renamed IDB Lab in 2018. Current APMG list: **AfDB, ADB, EBRD, IDB, IsDB, World Bank Group**, part-funded by **PPIAF** (`company_profile.md` Rule 6). Do not silently drop AfDB either.
- ❌ Never guarantee exam results, career outcomes, or project success.
- ❌ No unverifiable superlatives. "ATO APMG pertama di Indonesia" and "entitas pemerintah pertama di dunia yang menjadi accredited trainer CP3P" are factual claims and are allowed **with a source**.
- ❌ No programme-dump — pick the **1** relevant anchor per article.
- ❌ Never disparage other training providers to elevate the Institute.
- ✅ Every article cites ≥1–2 authoritative external sources. Any CP3P scope claim traces to **APMG**, never a reseller.
- ✅ Keep "infrastruktur / KPBU / PPP / PT PII" context near any mention of "IIGF" so it is never confused with the International Institute of Green Finance.
- ✅ Teach thoroughly and vendor-neutrally first; the programme anchor appears once, at the close.
- ✅ IIGF Institute framed consistently with `company_profile.md`.
