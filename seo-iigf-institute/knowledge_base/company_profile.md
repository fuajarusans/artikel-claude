## Positioning — what IIGF Institute actually is

IIGF Institute is the **knowledge arm of PT PII**, bridging the practice of infrastructure guarantees and financing with academia, policymakers, and practitioners. As a **think-tank** it produces research and publications; as an **education body** it runs training, certification, and online learning; as an **advocacy node** it facilitates policy dialogue and cross-stakeholder collaboration.

Its purpose: build a better and more sustainable infrastructure-development ecosystem in Indonesia by raising human-capital competence, strengthening the KPBU/PPP knowledge base, and encouraging policy that supports private participation and creative financing.

It positions itself as the **Center of Excellence** for KPBU/PPP in Indonesia — connecting international standards (e.g. the APMG PPP framework) with the realities of national and regional infrastructure development.

## Program CP3P (flagship — handle with care)

**CP3P** (*Certified Public-Private Partnership Professional*) is a professional certification from **APMG International**, the standard credential for demonstrating competence against international PPP good practice. In APMG's own wording the programme is *an innovation of* six multilateral development banks — **AfDB, ADB, EBRD, IDB, IsDB, and the World Bank Group** — **part-funded by PPIAF**. (Verified 2026-08-12 against `ppp-certification.com` home, `/about-us`, `/ppp-certification-guide/about-ppp-guide`, and the APMG product page — all four agree.)

> **Never write "MIF" in this list.** The *Multilateral Investment Fund* appears only in the **legacy foreword** of the PPP Certification Guide (2016 wording, which also predates AfDB's inclusion). MIF was renamed **IDB Lab** in October 2018 and now sits inside the IDB Group — which is why every current APMG page drops it and names **AfDB** instead. Old PDFs, resellers, and search snippets still circulate the MIF list; do not copy it back in. Equally, do not "play safe" by dropping AfDB: an intersection of the old and new lists matches **no** APMG source.

**IIGF Institute's role**: Accredited Training Organization (ATO) of APMG, the first in Indonesia since 2019, and recorded as the first government entity in the world to become an accredited trainer for CP3P. Training is delivered by APMG-certified trainers.

**Three certification levels** (fees indicative per 2026 publication — always verify and label):

| Level | Cakupan (PPP Guide) | Biaya (indikatif 2026) |
|---|---|---|
| Foundation | Bab 1 — konsep dasar & karakteristik PPP (prasyarat level lanjutan) | Rp 19.000.000 |
| Preparation (Practitioner) | Bab 2–5 — penyiapan proyek PPP | Rp 21.000.000 |
| Execution (Practitioner) | Bab 5–8 — strukturisasi, tender, kontrak & manajemen | Rp 21.000.000 |

Kandidat yang lulus ketiga ujian berhak menyandang kredensial CP3P.

> **Nuance writers must preserve**: passing Foundation alone does not confer the full CP3P credential. Certification demonstrates command of an international framework — it is not a guarantee of project success, and the framework still needs adapting to Indonesian KPBU regulation. **All syllabus/level claims must trace to APMG, never to a reseller.**

## Hubungan dengan PT PII

IIGF Institute is **not a standalone entity** — it is a work unit / knowledge-management arm of PT PII. The name "IIGF" comes from PT PII's international name, *Indonesia Infrastructure Guarantee Fund*. Its capacity, expertise, and credibility are rooted in PT PII's experience as the only BUMN mandated to guarantee KPBU infrastructure projects under the Ministry of Finance.

> **Disambiguation (critical for SEO/GEO):** the acronym "IIGF" is also used by unrelated organisations, e.g. the **International Institute of Green Finance** (China). In the Indonesian context, IIGF = Indonesia Infrastructure Guarantee Fund = PT PII, and IIGF Institute is its knowledge unit. **Always keep "infrastruktur / KPBU / PPP / PT PII" context near any mention of "IIGF".**

## Blacklist (do NOT say)

- ❌ **Names of any individual** — personnel, peneliti, pengurus, trainer, or PT PII Direksi/Komisaris. Use institutional framing ("tim riset IIGF Institute", "trainer tersertifikasi APMG").
- ❌ That IIGF Institute **owns, awards, issues, or accredits** the CP3P credential. APMG International does; IIGF Institute delivers the training as an ATO.
- ❌ That IIGF Institute is a **university**, a degree-granting body, or an **independent legal entity**. It is a work unit of PT PII.
- ❌ Any **fee or schedule without** an "indikatif" label **and** its reference year.
- ❌ A hard number for UNIID/JPII membership. Use the range with its year ("33–35 universitas, per publikasi 2026").
- ❌ Claiming a JIPM indexing status (Scopus, SINTA tier, etc.) that the researcher has not verified.
- ❌ That IIGF Institute or PT PII is an **anggota / member of Kantor Bersama KPBU**. The official list is 7 kementerian/lembaga and includes neither (Rule 5). Use "berkolaborasi dalam ekosistem Kantor Bersama KPBU".
- ❌ **MIF** (Multilateral Investment Fund) in the CP3P developer list — legacy 2016 wording, now IDB Lab (Rule 6). Current list: AfDB, ADB, EBRD, IDB, IsDB, World Bank Group + PPIAF as part-funder.
- ❌ Confusing **IIGF** with the International Institute of Green Finance or any other same-acronym organisation.
- ❌ Unverifiable superlatives ("terbaik", "pelatihan KPBU nomor satu"). The factual claims *"ATO APMG pertama di Indonesia"* and *"entitas pemerintah pertama di dunia yang menjadi accredited trainer CP3P"* are allowed **with a source**.
- ❌ Guaranteeing exam outcomes, career results, or project success from taking a course.
- ❌ Speaking on behalf of the government or PT PII on policy that IIGF Institute does not set.
