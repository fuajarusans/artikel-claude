## Universal rules (apply to every keyword — listed or not)
- ❌ Don't claim the client does something it doesn't (keep framing accurate).
- ❌ No unverifiable superlatives ("terbaik", "nomor satu").
- ❌ Don't invent product names, specs, versions, dates, pricing, or client outcomes — researcher verifies each.
- ❌ No service-dump — pick the **1** relevant anchor per article.
- ✅ Every article cites ≥1–2 authoritative external sources.
- ✅ Client framed consistently with `company_profile.md`.
