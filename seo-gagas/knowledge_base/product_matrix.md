## IMPORTANT NOTES (read before writing these clusters)

**(i) CNG cluster — "CNG Indonesia" & "Perusahaan CNG Indonesia" SHARE the same landing page (`perusahaan-cng-indonesia`).** Treat them as **ONE CNG cluster** and **dedup across both**: do not produce two articles covering the same ground. Split by **search intent** — "CNG Indonesia" leans **informational** (educate "apa itu CNG / cara kerja / manfaat / CNG vs LNG"); "Perusahaan CNG Indonesia" leans **commercial/comparison** (memilih & menilai penyedia CNG). The strategist must enforce angle_hash + subtopic dedup spanning both keywords so the cluster reads as complementary, not duplicate.

**(ii) Verify Gagas's LNG positioning before any "LNG company" framing.** The company profile lists **LNG Hub** only as a piece of infrastructure — it does **not** establish Gagas as a full LNG producer/trader. For "Perusahaan LNG Indonesia," teach LNG generally and vendor-neutrally; anchor Gagas through its **beyond-pipeline gas-bumi distribution and LNG Hub role within the PGN / Subholding Gas Pertamina ecosystem**, and have the researcher confirm the exact LNG role before the writer asserts it. When in doubt, frame Gagas as a **CNG/gas-bumi distributor that operates an LNG Hub**, not as an "LNG company."
