# Article notes

## Angle summary
This how-to article addresses industrial operators and facility managers at the decision/onboarding stage: it profiles which sectors are best-suited for Gaslink (manufacturing, HOREKA/hospitality, hospitals, laundry), explains why (continuous heat demand, stable volume, off-grid location), and walks through the seven-step onboarding process from application to first delivery. Distinct from gaslink-01 (informational/definitional) and gaslink-02 (cost comparison); this angle fills the operational/practical gap — "my sector matches, so what's next?"

## Subtopics covered
- Sector-specific suitability criteria: heat application, consumption patterns, accessibility
- Profiling of four sectors: manufaktur, hotel/HOREKA, rumah sakit, laundry komersial
- Equipment mapping (boiler, oven, water heater, sterilization) per sector
- Onboarding checklist: location readiness (Pressure Reduction Station, GTM access, retrofit compatibility)
- Seven-step numbered onboarding process (survey → agreement → installation → commissioning → go-live)
- Honest counter-conventional angle: conditions where Gaslink is NOT yet suitable (very small consumption, no local SPBG, limited space)
