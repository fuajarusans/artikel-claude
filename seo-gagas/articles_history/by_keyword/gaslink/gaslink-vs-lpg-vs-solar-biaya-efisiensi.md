# Article notes

## Angle summary
This article teaches industrial decision-makers how to compare energy costs fairly across three fuel sources (Gaslink/CNG, LPG, solar) by normalizing to a common energy basis (MMBTU), rather than comparing incompatible units. It covers calorific values, efficiency factors, total cost of ownership, payback methodology, and honesty-driven thresholds (when gas is NOT cheaper). Distinct from gaslink-01 (definitional/beyond-pipeline framing) and sibling gaslink-03 (how-to for specific sectors); this angle is comparison/decision-stage content addressing procurement teams' cost analysis needs.

## Subtopics covered
- Why comparing per-liter/per-kg/per-m³ is misleading; normalizing to MMBTU
- Calorific values (nilai kalori) for solar, LPG, CNG
- Efficiency factors and emission profiles (CO₂ per MMBTU)
- Harga Gas Bumi Tertentu (HGBT) framework and 7-sector scope
- Payback period concept and ambition volume thresholds
- Honest counter-conventional analysis: when gas is NOT cheaper
