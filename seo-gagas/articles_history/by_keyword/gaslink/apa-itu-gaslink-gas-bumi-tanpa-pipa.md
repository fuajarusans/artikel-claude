# Article notes

## Angle summary

This cornerstone article introduces the Gaslink service from a foundational, decision-useful perspective. It answers the question "what is Gaslink and what problem does it solve?" for decision-makers (factory managers, hotel operators, hospitals, laundries) who know gas bumi is cleaner and potentially cheaper than LPG/solar, but don't understand how gas can reach locations without pipeline infrastructure. The article focuses on the **beyond-pipeline concept** (serving customers outside pipeline-networked zones), the **end-to-end supply chain** (Mother Station → compression → GTM → Pressure Reduction Station → burner/boiler), and an **honest "when Gaslink is NOT yet suitable"** section. It deliberately does NOT cover deep cost comparisons (left for Gaslink-02, comparison angle) or sector-specific implementation guides (left for Gaslink-03, how-to angle).

## Subtopics covered

- CNG (Compressed Natural Gas) definition and compression mechanics
- Beyond-pipeline concept as "virtual gas pipeline" via mobile infrastructure
- Supply chain: Mother Station, gas compression, Gas Transport Module (GTM), Pressure Reduction Station, customer equipment
- Gaslink GTM (high-volume, dedicated Pressure Reduction Station) vs. Gaslink Cylinder (small-volume, space-limited) vs. pipeline gas (most economical if available)
- Honest contraindications: consumption too small, location already served by pipa, land constraints, volatile consumption patterns, coverage not available yet
- Safety properties of CNG (lighter than air, higher auto-ignition temperature, narrow flammability range)
- Smart metering / consumption-based billing
- Audience segments: manufacturing, HOREKA (hotel, restaurant, catering), healthcare, commercial laundry, UMKM
