# Article notes

## Angle summary
Process explainer that narrates how CNG distribution works end-to-end in Indonesia's archipelago geography: Mother Station compresses gas, kaskade tube trailer or GTM transports it, Pressure Reduction Station lowers pressure at customer site, and for inter-island logistics, CNG vessels carry compressed gas across sea routes. This answers the foundational question "how does gas reach areas with no pipeline?" with concrete Philippine geography, role of natural gas for remote power generation (PLN Batam, Gresik–Lombok), and honest limits of the technology (distance economics, land requirements, logistics dependencies).

## Subtopics covered
- Beyond-pipeline konsep distribusi gas tanpa jaringan pipa permanen
- Virtual pipeline: kaskade tabung, tube trailer, GTM (Gas Transport Module)
- Infrastruktur: Mother Station, daughter station, Pressure Reduction Station (PRS)
- Geografi kepulauan Indonesia: pipa nasional hanya 22.821 km, terkonsentrasi Sumatra–Jawa
- CNG vessel: kapal angkutan CNG antar pulau, contoh Jayanti Baruna Gresik–Lombok September 2024
- Aplikasi nyata: PLN Batam 2013 (6 MW), PLTMGU Lombok Peaker 2024 (23.38 MMSCF)
- Keandalan pasokan: logistik gudang, stok tabung, cadangan armada
- Kapan CNG truk BUKAN jawaban terbaik: jarak ekonomis, kebutuhan baseline tinggi, lokasi sudah terlayani pipa
