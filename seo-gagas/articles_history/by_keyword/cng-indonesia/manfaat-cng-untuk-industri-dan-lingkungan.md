# Article notes

## Angle summary
Benefit-driven article that separates operational benefits (thermal efficiency, stable calorific value, longer equipment life, lower maintenance) from environmental benefits (lower CO2, NOx, particulate emissions) and explicitly adds a counter-conventional "honest" section on when CNG is NOT optimal for a given operation. This teaches decision-makers that "cleaner ≠ always cost-effective" and "lower emissions ≠ zero lockdown risk," grounding the narrative in industrial pragmatism rather than environmental advocacy alone.

## Subtopics covered
- Efisiensi pembakaran: nilai kalori stabil, panas lebih terukur vs solar
- Emisi lebih rendah: CO2 52.91 kg/MMBTU vs solar 74.14 (29% lebih rendah), NOx 75–94% lebih rendah (kendaraan), partikulat PM berkurang
- Peralatan lebih awet: pembakaran lebih bersih, residu lebih sedikit, siklus servis lebih panjang
- Jejak karbon lifecycle: EIA data CO2 per satuan energi
- Sektor aplikasi: boiler, oven/furnace, genset, pembangkit listrik
- Kapan CNG BELUM cocok: lokasi sudah dilintasi pipa (pilih pipa), jarak terlalu jauh, kebutuhan sangat rendah
- Case study: RS Hasan Sadikin Bandung Rp 3 miliar/tahun hemat via Gaslink (Agustus 2025)
- Hedging pada angka "68% hemat vs solar" — ditampilkan sebagai klaim Gagas/KADIN plus independent range 30–50%
