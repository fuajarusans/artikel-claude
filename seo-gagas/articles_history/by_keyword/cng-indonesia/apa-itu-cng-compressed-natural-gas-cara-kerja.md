# Article notes

## Angle summary
Cornerstone definitional article on CNG as a technology — explains what CNG is (compressed natural gas, methane ~90–95% composition, at 200–250 bar pressure), why it matters (volume compresses to ~1/250, enabling beyond-pipeline distribution), and how it differs from gas pipa (same source gas, different pressure) and LPG (different chemical source: propane/butane). This is the foundational concept article for the entire CNG Indonesia cluster, teaching the physics and chemistry vendor-neutrally before downstream articles address comparisons, benefits, distribution infrastructure, and energy policy.

## Subtopics covered
- CNG definition: Compressed Natural Gas, metana CH4 composition
- Tekanan kompresi: 200–250 bar, volume menyusut ~1/250
- 7-langkah proses kompresi: ekstraksi → pemurnian → kompresi Mother Station → penyimpanan → transportasi virtual pipeline → penurunan tekanan PRS → pemakaian
- Perbedaan CNG vs gas pipa: asal sama (metana), distribusi beda (compressed vs pipa permanen)
- Perbedaan CNG vs LPG: asal beda (gas bumi vs propana/butana), wujud beda (gas vs cair)
- Perbedaan CNG vs LNG: cara pemadatan (kompresi vs pendinginan -162°C)
- Beyond pipeline: konsep distribusi gas tanpa jaringan pipa permanen
- BBG (Bahan Bakar Gas) sebutan Indonesia untuk CNG
- Contoh nyata: PLN Batam sejak 2013, Gresik→Lombok 2016
