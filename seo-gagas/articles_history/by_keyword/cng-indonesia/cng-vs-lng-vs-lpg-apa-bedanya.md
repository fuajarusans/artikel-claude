# Article notes

## Angle summary
Comparison explainer that distinguishes three gas types on four dimensions (asal/origin, wujud/form, tekanan/pressure, nilai kalori/energy density) and provides a practical decision framework for when to use each. Unlike generic "here are the differences" articles, this teaches that the choice depends on volume, distance, application (household vs industrial vs power generation), and infrastructure investment. Explicitly covers HGBT (Harga Gas Bumi Tertentu) policy for 7 industrial sectors, addressing the why of cost differences per unit of energy rather than raw per-kg/per-liter confusion.

## Subtopics covered
- Tiga jenis gas: asal (gas bumi vs samping kilang), wujud (gas vs cair), tekanan penyimpanan
- CNG: dikompresi 200–250 bar, tetap gas, metana dari gas bumi
- LNG: didinginkan -162°C, dicairkan, metana dari gas bumi, volume menyusut ~1/600
- LPG: propana/butana dari kilang minyak, cair, tekanan 5–10 bar
- Nilai kalori (calorific value): perbandingan per massa vs per volume
- Kerangka keputusan: kapan pakai CNG (dekat-menengah), LNG (volume besar jarak jauh), LPG (skala kecil)
- HGBT 7 sektor industri pupuk/petrokimia/oleokimia/baja/keramik/kaca/sarung tangan karet
- Perubahan pasar energi 2025–2026: 80–84% LPG harus diimpor
- Perbedaan esensial: gas bumi (CNG/LNG) vs produk samping kilang (LPG)
