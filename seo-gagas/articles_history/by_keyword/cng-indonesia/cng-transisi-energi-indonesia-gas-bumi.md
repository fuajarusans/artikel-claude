# Article notes

## Angle summary
Thought-leadership explainer that positions CNG within Indonesia's energy transition narrative: bridge fuel that can displace coal and LPG in the near-to-medium term (next 20–30 years) as renewable energy scales up. Teaches the macro-level energy mix targets (gas 24% of national energy mix by 2050, 12 BSCFD by 2030, NZE 2060), the role as baseload backup when wind/solar fluctuates, and explicitly acknowledges limitations (still fossil fuel, methane GWP20 82.5x CO2, carbon lock-in risks). This makes the article credible by avoiding one-sided advocacy.

## Subtopics covered
- Energi transisi (bridge fuel) definisi dan peran
- Bauran energi nasional target: gas 24% pada 2050, minyak 20%, EBT 31% (RUEN)
- Persentase saat ini: EBT 15.75% 2025, target 23% 2025 (Kementerian ESDM)
- Target produksi gas: 12 BSCFD pada 2030 (Permen ESDM 35/2021)
- Net Zero Emission 2060 target pemerintah
- Baseload backup peran gas ketika EBT (surya/angin) berfluktuasi
- Peran CNG spesifik: penyalur gas ke daerah tanpa pipa, mendukung desentralisasi energi
- Batas dan risiko: gas tetap fosil, metana GWP20 82.5x CO2 (20 tahun), carbon lock-in risks
- Sisi kritis: mengapa gas bukan solusi akhir tetapi jembatan
- Perbandingan dengan negara lain transisi energi pragmatis
