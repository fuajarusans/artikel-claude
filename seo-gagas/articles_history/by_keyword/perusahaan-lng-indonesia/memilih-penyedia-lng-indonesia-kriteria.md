# Article notes

## Angle summary
This is the *vendor-neutral buyer's guide* for selecting an LNG provider/solution in the beyond-pipeline context. The key framing is that "perusahaan LNG" masks three distinct roles: *hulu* (kilang/liquefaction), *midstream* (FSRU/regasification/logistics), and *hilir* (distribution/LNG Hub). A buyer beyond-pipeline needs someone in the *hilir* distribution role, not a producer. The article establishes five core evaluation criteria (legality/business license, cold-chain reliability, cryogenic safety/ISO certification, ecosystem completeness, transparency/SLA), then translates each into a checklist of concrete questions to ask a prospective provider. A three-role diagram and honest "jebakan" (pitfall) section on vendor confusion / hidden costs round out the trust-building frame. Gagas is positioned credibly at the hilir level with ISO credentials, not as a full LNG company.

## Subtopics covered
- Three-role distinction:
  - Hulu (kilang): liquefies gas bumi to −162°C, ~1/600 shrinkage
  - Midstream (FSRU/regasification, logistics): kapal, terminal, regasifikasi
  - Hilir (distribution): LNG Hub, break-bulking, ISO tank, last-mile to customer
- For beyond-pipeline customers, *hilir role is what you need* — you don't need a full LNG company
- Five core criteria:
  1. Legality: active business license (Izin Usaha Niaga Gas) from ESDM
  2. Cold-chain reliability: adequate armada, proven delivery schedule
  3. Cryogenic safety & certification: ISO 9001 (quality), ISO 45001 (K3/safety), Permen ESDM compliance, BOG procedures
  4. Ecosystem completeness: access to LNG source, ISO tank fleet, regasification support, 24-hour technical backup
  5. Transparency: clear volume accounting (boil-off allocation), SLA, who owns which stage
- Pitfalls to avoid: chasing lowest price without total cost / supply reliability; assuming all "perusahaan LNG" have same capability; ignoring who is responsible for regasification (SRU at site); neglecting ecosystem breadth (isolated vs connected to wider supply)
- Checklist of 7 questions: legalitas, cold chain, LNG source, regasification at site, safety/cert, volume transparency, ecosystem/backup capacity
