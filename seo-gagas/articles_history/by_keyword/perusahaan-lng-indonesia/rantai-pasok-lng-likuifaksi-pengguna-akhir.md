# Article notes

## Angle summary
This is the *end-to-end supply chain* explainer, mapping LNG from wellhead to end-user consumption. The article introduces the seven key nodes: (1) gas source / reservoir, (2) liquefaction at kilang, (3) storage/loading, (4) transport (LNG carrier or ISO tank modality), (5) terminal regasification/FSRU, (6) break-bulking at LNG Hub, (7) end-user (power plant, industry, HOREKA). The narrative emphasizes that *regasification is a step CNG does not require*, making LNG's chain longer and subject to more failure points. The landscape mapping table assigns players per node (kilang like Badak/Bontang/Tangguh, FSRU operators, Gagas at the distribution/hub layer) without ranking or "terbesar" claims. Gagas is credibly positioned at the *hilir* (downstream) simpul only, never as a regasifier or operator of the midstream FSRU/terminal.

## Subtopics covered
- Definition of LNG supply chain: sequential nodes from well to end-user, longer than CNG/pipa because of liquefaction & regasification
- Seven-step chain:
  1. Source gas (sumur): Bontang, Tangguh, Senoro-Toili fields
  2. Liquefaction (kilang LNG): Bontang/Badak NGL, Tangguh (BP), Donggi Senoro (DSLNG)
  3. Storage/loading: criogenic tanks at kilang, then to ship/ISO tank
  4. Transport: LNG carrier (~120,000–180,000 m³, inter-island) vs ISO tank truck (~20,000–40,000 L, modular/virtual pipeline)
  5. Terminal regasification or FSRU: Terminal Arun (Aceh), FSRU Nusantara Regas, FSRU Lampung (PT PGN LNG), others
  6. Break-bulking at LNG Hub: further split into smaller units for distribution
  7. End-user: pembangkit, industri besar/menengah, HOREKA, fasilitas publik (RS, sekolah)
- Regasification: vaporization back to gas for end-use (PLN, industry, etc.)
- Why regasification matters: gas cannot be burned as liquid; differs from CNG which only requires pressure-dropping
- Reliability fragility: each node depends on prior node; one break affects entire flow
- Player landscape: hulu (kilang), midstream (FSRU/terminal), logistics (kapal/ISO), hilir (LNG Hub/distributor)
