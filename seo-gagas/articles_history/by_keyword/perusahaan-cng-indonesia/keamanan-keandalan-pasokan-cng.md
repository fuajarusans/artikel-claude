# Article notes

## Angle summary
Commercial assurance article that demystifies "keamanan" and "keandalan" by mapping them to verifiable standards and operational practices. Keamanan = ISO 45001, Permen ESDM 32/2021, Kepmen 176.K/2024 SMKM. Keandalan = supply continuity, backup Mother Station capacity, redundant GTM fleet, telemetry monitoring, contractual SLA. The article doesn't claim one penyedia is safer than another (vendor-neutral) but gives the buyer the technical vocabulary and checklist to verify claims independently.

## Subtopics covered
- Keamanan distribusi CNG: ISO 45001:2018 standar K3
- Permen ESDM 32/2021: inspeksi teknis instalasi migas berkala
- Kepmen ESDM 176.K/2024: SMKM sistem manajemen keselamatan migas (effective 25 Jan 2025)
- Auto-ignition temperature metana: 537–540°C, lebih tinggi dari bensin/solar
- Flammability range: 5–15% konsentrasi di udara
- Keandalan pasokan: 99.9% target operational, Mother Station capacity backup
- GTM cadangan armada untuk kontinuitas distribusi
- Pressure Relief Device: proteksi tekanan berlebih
- Telemetri dan monitoring real-time sistem tekanan/volume
- SLA kontrak jangka waktu respons dan tanggung jawab penyedia
- Pembeda: standar kendaraan (ISO 11439) vs standar operasi distribusi (ISO 45001)
