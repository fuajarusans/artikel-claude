# Article notes

## Angle summary
Vendor-neutral buyer's guide that frames CNG provider evaluation as a 7-point due-diligence framework: legal status (IUN dari Kementerian ESDM), safety certifications (ISO 45001:2018, ISO 9001:2015), operational compliance (Permen ESDM 32/2021 inspeksi teknis), supply redundancy, cost predictability, and track record. Unlike competitor blogs that pitch their own services, this article positions the reader as the evaluator and provides actionable criteria without naming competing vendors or claiming rankings.

## Subtopics covered
- Izin Usaha Niaga (IUN) Kementerian ESDM wajib untuk niaga CNG
- Hak Khusus BPH Migas instrumen terpisah untuk penyedia jaringan
- ISO 45001:2018 standar manajemen K3 untuk distribusi gas
- ISO 9001:2015 standar manajemen mutu
- Permen ESDM 32/2021 inspeksi teknis instalasi migas berkala
- Keandalan pasokan: Mother Station capacity, GTM cadangan, backup moda
- Penghematan yang terverifikasi: 15–40% range (tergantung bahan bakar acuan)
- Kontrak SLA jangka waktu tanggapan dan tanggung jawab
- Virtual pipeline dan teknologi distribusi
- 7 kriteria framework evaluasi
