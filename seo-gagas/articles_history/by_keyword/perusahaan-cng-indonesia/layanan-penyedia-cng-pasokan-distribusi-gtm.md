# Article notes

## Angle summary
Service scope explainer that clarifies what "berlangganan CNG" actually means: not just buying gas, but purchasing a bundled service that includes reliable source supply, GTM distribution, technical commissioning, ongoing telemetry/monitoring, and 24-hour support. A table delineates responsibilities: what buyer must provide (site prep, equipment, permits) vs. what penyedia guarantees (supply continuity, equipment monitoring, emergency response). This prevents contract surprise and educates C-suite stakeholders that CNG is infrastructure, not commodity.

## Subtopics covered
- Layanan penyedia CNG: pasokan gas + distribusi + support teknis bundel
- Gas Transport Module (GTM): tube trailer, kapasitas, scheduling
- Pressure Reduction Station (PRS): tekanan 0.8–1.5 bar (industri standard)
- C-Cyl: silinder 28 m3 per cylinder penyimpanan di lokasi
- Mother Station: sumber gas kompresi awal
- Telemetri / smart meter: penagihan berbasis pemakaian real-time
- Dukungan teknis: commissioning, emergency response, maintenance monitoring
- SLA dan tanggung jawab: apa yang dijamin penyedia vs buyer
- Kontrak jangka panjang dan fleksibilitas
- Cost structure: pasokan vs distribusi vs support sebagai line items terpisah
