# Article notes

## Angle summary
Inverted framework that teaches via negative example: instead of "7 kriteria positif" (perusahaan-cng-indonesia-01), this covers "7 kesalahan pembeli" with consequences and mitigation. Unique angle: includes mistakes FROM THE BUYER'S SIDE (underestimating commissioning time, ignoring equipment retrofit compatibility) not just "vendor wrong choice." This balanced view (you can choose the right penyedia, but if YOU don't prepare properly, CNG still fails) is rarely found in competitor articles and builds trust through candor.

## Subtopics covered
- 7 kesalahan memilih penyedia CNG dan solusi
- Jebakan harga termurah vs total cost ownership (TCO)
- Mengabaikan legalitas izin (IUN, Hak Khusus BPH Migas)
- Tidak verifikasi sertifikasi (ISO 45001, Permen ESDM 32/2021)
- Underestimasi kebutuhan infrastruktur lokasi (lahan, kedalaman tanah, dll)
- Mengabaikan dukungan teknis purnajual (24 jam, spare parts, maintenance)
- Hidden costs dalam kontrak (baseline vs variable, emergency surcharge)
- Kesalahan pembeli: ketidaksiapan, ketergesa-gesaan, hidden demand mismatch
- Klaim "24 jam" tanpa SLA terukur vs kontrak jelas
- Hidden cost dan biaya tambahan di luar kontrak
