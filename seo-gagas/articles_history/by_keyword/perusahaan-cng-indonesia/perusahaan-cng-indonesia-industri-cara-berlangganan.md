# Article notes

## Angle summary
Sector profiling + onboarding how-to that teaches which industries benefit from CNG (HGBT 7 sektor: pupuk, petrokimia, oleokimia, baja, keramik, kaca, sarung tangan karet; plus HOREKA) and walks through the 6-step subscription process: site survey, equipment appraisal, regulatory compliance, technical setup, test run, go-live. Explicitly includes a "when your business is NOT ready" section to manage expectations and avoid false leads.

## Subtopics covered
- Sektor HGBT 7: pupuk, petrokimia, oleokimia, baja, keramik, kaca, sarung tangan karet
- Sektor HOREKA: hotel, restoran, laundry F&B (non-HGBT)
- Konsumsi minimum: C-Cyl ~300 m3/bulan threshold untuk ekonomis GTM
- Profil sektor: boiler, oven/furnace, genset aplikasi
- Survei lokasi: jarak ke Mother Station, infrastruktur lokasi, retrofit kebutuhan
- Onboarding 6 langkah: survey → engineering → permit → install → test → operasi
- Retrofit burner dan equipment adjustment untuk setiap sektor
- Kontrak jangka panjang dan komitmen volume
- Kapan belum cocok: jarak terlalu jauh, kebutuhan musiman, lokasi sudah ada pipa
