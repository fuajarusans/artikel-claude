# Article notes

## Angle summary
This counter-conventional decision-stage article takes the unusual stance of urging readers NOT to convert — or at least to pause and self-assess — rather than pushing all toward Gasku adoption. It answers the unasked question: what conditions make BBG conversion a bad idea? Five concrete criteria (low daily use, no nearby SPBG, aging vehicle, baggage constraints, active manufacturer warranty) are presented with honest acknowledgment of trade-offs (power loss ~10–15%, space sacrifice, warranty cancellation risk) vs. legitimate safety facts. This builds trust by positioning PGN Gagas as rational advisor, not overseller. Complements gasku-02 (positive case) with healthy skepticism; distinct from gasku-01 (safety myths) by focusing on **operational/economic negation rather than technical safety**.

## Subtopics covered
- Five hard criteria for "not yet suitable": low consumption, distance from SPBG, vehicle age/condition, baggage needs, active warranty
- Trade-offs vs myths: real power loss (10–15% normal, ~20% at full load) vs. overblown "meledak" fears
- Warranty cancellation risk: genuine concern requiring pabrikan confirmation
- Space/volume constraints: tabung CNG footprint and Gaslink Cylinder alternative for tight spaces
- SPBG availability limitations by region (14 stations / 7 provinces as of Nov 2025)
- Honest checklist: five green-light conditions where conversion IS sensible despite trade-offs
- Decision pathway: conditions matrix (fit profile → recalculate payback → reassess)
