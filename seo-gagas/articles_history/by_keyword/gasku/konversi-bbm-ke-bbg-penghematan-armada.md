# Article notes

## Angle summary
This decision-stage guide addresses fleet owners and operations managers who understand Gasku basics (gasku-01) but need concrete numbers and next steps: how to calculate savings, payback timelines, converter kit investment, HowTo onboarding for multi-vehicle operations, and government program context. Covers the economic case with honest ranges (not single headline figures), breakeven thresholds, and government conversion support (Bengkel Keliling, CSR units). Complements gasku-01 (what + how) with cost/investment answer, and gasku-03 (negation/decision criteria) by pushing the positive case forward.

## Subtopics covered
- Three-variable payback framework: daily mileage, fuel consumption, price differential
- Savings calculation methodology with illustrative examples per fleet profile
- Converter Kit cost range (market span, not Gagas tariff)
- Payback period calculations at various consumption scenarios
- Government program context: Perpres 64/2012, Kemenhub/ESDM support, Bengkel Keliling BBG, CSR converter units
- HowTo six-step process: audit → shop selection → converter type choice → installation → testing → SPBG registration
- Honest caveats: warranty risk, maintenance cost inclusion, SPBG availability constraints, price volatility

## Sections (H2s)
1. "Apakah Konversi ke BBG Layak Secara Ekonomi untuk Armada Anda?" — framing: feasibility depends on three variables (daily mileage, fuel consumption, BBM–LSP price spread); high-use fleets are best candidates
2. "Berapa Penghematan yang Bisa Anda Harapkan? Begini Cara Menghitungnya" — kerangka (framework): daily consumption × price differential × days per month → monthly savings; worked examples for different fleet profiles; explicitly labeled as illustrative, not guaranteed
