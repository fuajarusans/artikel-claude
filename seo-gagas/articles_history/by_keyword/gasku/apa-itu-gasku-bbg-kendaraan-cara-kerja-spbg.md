# Article notes

## Angle summary
This is the cornerstone informational/definitional explainer for the Gasku keyword cluster. It answers awareness-stage questions: what is Gasku (BBG/CNG for vehicles), how it differs from gasoline, how the Bi-Fuel system and SPBG network work, safety facts (autoignition temps, tabung certification), and common misconceptions. Intentionally narrowed to definitional scope, leaving cost calculations (gasku-02) and decision criteria/negation (gasku-03) to siblings. Distinct from gaslink-01 (industrial/beyond-pipeline) by focus on **transportation sector and point-of-sale (SPBG) mechanics**.

## Subtopics covered
- Definition: Gasku as CNG-based BBG from PGN Gagas for vehicle transport
- BBG vs BBM comparison: source, octane, emissions, price per LSP
- CNG technical basics: ~200 bar compression, RON >120, 100% domestic sourcing
- Bi-Fuel system: two-tank, two-regulator, manual/automatic switching, fallback to gasoline
- SPBG network operation: gas compression, filtering, dispensing, safety systems
- Safety/myths: tabung durability (impact/bonfire/gunfire rated), autoignition thresholds (~540°C CNG vs ~360°C gasoline), pressure regulation, leak dispersion
- Liter Setara Premium (LSP) satuan equivalence concept
