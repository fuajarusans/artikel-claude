## 5. Algorithm context the editor must enforce (from the report's "Findings")

- **AI Overviews & zero-click**: studies cited in the report note organic CTR drops >60% on informational queries as users get answers from AI snippets. Win by being the cited source, not by chasing raw clicks on thin content.
- **March 2026 Core Update (27 Mar – 8 Apr 2026)** rewarded:
  - **Originality** — pages adding new information, primary data, or a unique perspective (not summaries of existing content).
  - **E-E-A-T** — clear expertise/authorship signals.
  - **AI content WITHOUT editorial oversight is penalized**; AI content WITH expert review is safe.
  - **Specific relevance** — content that truly answers a specific user need beats generic "helpful" filler.

This validates the entire pipeline philosophy: human-sounding, expert-reviewed, originally-angled, specifically-useful articles — never generic AI summaries.
