FIRST IN THE WORLD

ARC SUCCESSFULLY
REPRODUCES
TROPICAL EELS

The Aquaculture Research Center (ARC), a research and development
facility has recorded a historic moment in the field of aquaculture with
its success in the initial steps of reproducing the high-value tropical eel,
Anguilla bicolor.

     This research can help reduce dependence on the capture of
     Anguilla bicolor, which is 'Nearly Threatened' in the wild.
     The ARC successfully hatched 70,000 larvae with a larval survival
     rate over 11 days.
     This breakthrough supports sustainable aquaculture practices that
     can protect natural eel populations from overfishing."

updating aquaculture technology and anticipating threats to sustainable
aquaculture such as climate change, diseases.
Located in West Java, ATD develops a range of aquaculture technologies
designed to optimize operations for fish and shrimp farmers.

JAPFA Aquaculture Research Station (JARS) is our facilities that focus on conducting
research and development of the best feed formulations to support our shrimp farmers'.
Good feed formulation helps to maintain the water stability as it is an essential component
of aquaculture, especially when each area has different water conditions. Currently, JARS
operates in North Sumatra, West Java and East Java.
