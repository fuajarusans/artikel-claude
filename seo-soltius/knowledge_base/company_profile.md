"Largest revenue contributor to SAP Indonesia (since 2015)" is acceptable because it comes from the verified profile — but attribute it as a recognition, not a boast. Don't claim Soltius *builds* SAP software. Don't invent client outcomes, project values, or consultant counts beyond "200+".
