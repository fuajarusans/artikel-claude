# Article notes

## Angle summary

This pilot article uses a diagnostic checklist format (seven measurable signs with thresholds) to help SME decision-makers assess their readiness for an ERP system. Unlike definitional or capability-comparison articles, the angle is problem-centered and practitioner-grounded — it validates real bottlenecks (10+ day month-end close, Excel-as-database, unreconciled data) that trigger genuine ERP need, rather than positioning SAP Business One as a generic solution. The article deliberately includes an honest "you might NOT be ready yet" section that competitors avoid, building trust by acknowledging internal-readiness factors beyond technology. This positions Soltius as a thoughtful implementation partner, not a vendor pushing the biggest system.
