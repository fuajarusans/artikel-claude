> **Per-month keywords:** keywords are defined per month in `monthly_runs/<YYYY-MM>/keywords.md` and fully replace prior sets. The rows below are a **reference library for recurring/known topics**. > **Framing for this client:** PT PII is the **government-guarantee provider** — Badan Usaha Penjaminan Infrastruktur (BUPI), a BUMN Persero and Special Mission Vehicle of the Ministry of Finance. It **menjamin · menerbitkan penjaminan pemerintah · menstrukturkan penjaminan · mendampingi penyiapan dan transaksi proyek**. It is **not** a lender, an equity investor, a contractor, an operator, or a regulator. Max **2–3** client self-mentions per article — usually the closing plus at most one mid-article aside. Teach the topic generously and vendor-neutrally first; PT PII earns the CTA by being genuinely helpful.

## Universal rules (apply to every keyword — listed or not)

- ❌ Never frame PT PII as a lender, equity investor, contractor, operator, or regulator.
- ❌ Never state that PT PII sits under the Danantara holding.
- ❌ Never name a Direktur or Komisaris (hard blacklist — see `company_profile.md`).
- ❌ Never publish a figure without its reference year.
- ❌ Never cite a revoked or misnumbered regulation as current law.
- ❌ No unverifiable superlatives ("terbaik", "nomor satu"). The factual "satu-satunya BUMN dengan mandat BUPI" is allowed.
- ❌ No service-dump — pick the **1** relevant anchor per article.
- ❌ Never imply the guarantee removes the badan usaha's commercial risk, or that it is a subsidy.
- ✅ Every article cites ≥1–2 authoritative external sources (Kemenkeu/DJPPR, regulasi primer, Bappenas, World Bank/PPP Knowledge Lab, ADB, MIGA, BPS).
- ✅ Keep "infrastruktur / KPBU / Kementerian Keuangan" context near any mention of "IIGF" so it is never confused with the International Institute of Green Finance.
- ✅ PT PII framed consistently with `company_profile.md`.
- ✅ Teach vendor-neutrally first; the anchor appears once, in context, usually at the close.
