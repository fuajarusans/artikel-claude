# Article notes

## Angle summary
This article inverts the standard PPP explanatory frame: instead of describing what PPP is and how it works, it explains what credit committees actually evaluate before they fund a PPP project. The gap in existing content is acute: top results are either policy documents (why PPP matters), capability pages (law firms), or datasets (ADB monitor)—none address the practitioner question: "What do lenders actually check, and what makes them walk away?" The differentiator is the lender's-chair perspective: cash flow reliability, contract certainty, guarantee standing (Fitch rating of the guarantor), and the 12-month path to financial close. PT PII's credibility is concrete and verifiable: it is itself a rated counterparty (Fitch BBB/F2/AAA(idn) Stable as of 12 Feb 2026), so the guarantee is only as good as that standing.
