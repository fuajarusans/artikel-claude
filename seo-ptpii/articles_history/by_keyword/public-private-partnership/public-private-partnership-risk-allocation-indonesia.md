# Article notes

## Angle summary
This article addresses a critical gap in English-language PPP material targeting investors and credit analysts: the exact boundary between risks that are contractually the government's responsibility, risks that a government guarantee will cover, and risks that remain with the sponsor regardless. Most investment material on Indonesia's PPP framework explains process; almost none draws this operational line. The differentiator is the risk matrix alongside guarantee scope, showing which categories the government has accepted (and guarantor can reimburse) and which are explicitly out of scope—a distinction that determines bid pricing and lender appetite. PT PII's credibility lies in its role as the institution that says yes or no to guarantee requests, giving it unique visibility into misallocations that fail at credit committee.
