# Article notes

## Angle summary
Every planning document repeats the same sentence: infrastructure investment needs far exceed APBN capacity. What's rarely explained: if government covers only ~40%, where does the rest come from, through what instruments, and under what conditions? A credit analyst, project sponsor, or economic journalist trying to answer that today finds three types of pages: vendor-specific finance company marketing, government explanations still using 2015–2019 data, and instrument directories with names but no content. The differentiator is a vendor-neutral map of infrastructure funding composition by source (APBN, APBD, BUMN, private sector, multilateral) and where fiscal instruments like government guarantees work within that map. PT PII's credibility is its raison d'être: it was created precisely to bridge this funding gap through guarantee and guarantee-adjacent instruments, and as BUPI under the Ministry of Finance it sees both the demand and supply sides.
