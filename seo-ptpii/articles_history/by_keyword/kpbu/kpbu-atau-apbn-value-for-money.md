# Article notes

## Angle summary
The article reframes the KPBU vs conventional procurement decision around Value for Money rather than budget availability. It inverts a common field pattern: teams pick KPBU first (because APBD is tight), then spend years discovering transaction costs eroded the promised value. The differentiator is an honest decision framework that names when KPBU is NOT the right choice—deliberately giving equal or greater weight to the "when not to use it" section. No other competitive content on the SERP balances the two sides; most material is promotional or definitional. PT PII's credibility here comes from its role evaluating and structuring project risk, which gives it unique visibility into which projects fail the Value for Money test early.
