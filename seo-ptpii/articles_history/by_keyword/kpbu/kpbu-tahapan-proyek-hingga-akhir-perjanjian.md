# Article notes

## Angle summary
The article addresses the regulatory freshness of KPBU processes according to Perpres 38/2015 and Permen PPN/Bappenas 7/2023 (which superseded Permen 4/2015 and 2/2020 still circulating in search results). The differentiator is the six-stage framework (perencanaan → penyiapan → transaksi → konstruksi → operasi → berakhirnya perjanjian) with explicit outputs and decision gates at each stage, grounded in current implementing regulations. This distinct-from-history angle fills a gap: while most SERP content discusses process generically, none anchors the stages to the 2023 regulatory update or explains the concrete output requirements per stage.

## Subtopics covered
- Tahapan proyek KPBU (six stages with outputs)
- Perencanaan (studi pendahuluan, Daftar Rencana KPBU)
- Penyiapan (prastudi kelayakan, Value for Money)
- Transaksi (prakualifikasi, lelang, financial close)
- Konstruksi dan operasi
- Perpres 38/2015 + Permen 7/2023 regulatory framework
- PJPK (Penanggung Jawab Proyek Kerja Sama) definition and roles
- Simpul KPBU and Kantor Bersama KPBU (institutional structure)
- Aturan yang berubah setelah 2023 update
