# Article notes

## Angle summary
The article maps risk allocation in KPBU contracts ex-ante (before events occur, at the negotiation table). It takes the principle that each risk is assigned to the party best able to manage or absorb it, operationalizes it into the eleven-category national taxonomy (via Simpul KPBU), and calls out the three most expensive misallocations in practice: land-clearing delays treated as private responsibility, demand risk in fixed-tariff schemes, and political/policy-change risk. The differentiator is the concrete matriks showing allocation per risk category, which no competitive content on the SERP provides. It fills a gap at the practitioner negotiation table: guides explain the principle; this article explains the application.
