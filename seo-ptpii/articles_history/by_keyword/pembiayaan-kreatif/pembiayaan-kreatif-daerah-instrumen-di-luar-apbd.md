# Article notes

## Angle summary
When a regional budget shrinks, the typical reaction is "cross projects off the RPJMD list." But there are six distinct financing pathways beyond APBD alone, each with different rules, capacity, and readiness barriers. The gap in content is sharp: the top SERP is all Kementerian policy pages explaining why creative financing matters; none shows a comparison table of the six instruments side by side. Readers leave those articles with conceptual understanding and zero ability to choose. The differentiator is a vendor-neutral peta (map) of six instruments—KPBU daerah, pinjaman daerah, obligasi daerah, Land Value Capture, dana abadi daerah, Dukungan Kelayakan—with source, rules, and status-pemakaian (is any Indonesian region actually using this today?). PT PII's credibility is its daily engagement with project preparation and viability at the regional scale—the vantage point that sees both supply and demand.

## Subtopics covered
- Pembiayaan kreatif definition and scope
- KPBU daerah (regional PPP)
- Pinjaman daerah (regional borrowing)
- Obligasi daerah dan sukuk daerah (regional bonds)
- Land Value Capture / land monetization
- Dana abadi daerah (regional endowment funds)
- Dukungan Kelayakan / VGF
- Fiscal rules and budget constraints (UU 1/2022 HKPD)
- Status pemakaian (adoption status) of each instrument
