# Article notes

## Angle summary
A pattern repeats in many regions: a project is announced confidently, makes local news, then disappears—never formally cancelled, just stalled, usually between feasibility study and tender. A year later no one can explain exactly where it got stuck. This article serves a reader in the most uncomfortable condition: the project is already running and stuck, and they need to diagnose why before blaming the instrument itself. This is an angle the SERP systematically avoids. The entire top page carries Kementerian content that pushes adoption; no page discusses failure openly. But there is official backing for the diagnosis: DJPK Kementerian Keuangan stated on 26 May 2026 that the main barrier is technical readiness and institutional caution—this article operationalizes that into seven recognizable causes with gejala (symptoms), penyebab (root), and tindakan (corrective action). PT PII's credibility comes from its position seeing both sides: it sees projects that failed not from lack of funds but from unripe structure and documentation.
