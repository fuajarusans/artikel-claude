# Article notes

## Angle summary
A common field pattern: a regional meeting decides "let's use KPBU" in month one, then the project stalls in month eight because there is no feasibility study, no one is assigned to lead the project, and multi-year payment commitments were never discussed with the DPRD. The order is backwards. This article serves a reader at the decision point, not at the learning point: a Bappeda or OPD official already convinced they want creative financing and needing to know what must be satisfied first, in what order, and at which point the region is genuinely not ready. No SERP content provides this—all of it stops at the "why this matters" level. Operational readiness is the harder conversation. DJPK Kementerian Keuangan stated on 26 May 2026 that the main barrier is technical capacity, not instrument availability—this article operationalizes that statement with eight concrete prerequisites and honest signals of when to postpone.
