# Article notes

## Angle summary
The article addresses a critical misreading in the field: when a project gets a government guarantee, stakeholders assume all risk is now covered. Wrong. The guarantee is a secondary promise on the government's specific obligations in the contract, not on project success. This boundary determines bankability entirely—whether a lender will enter and at what tenor. The differentiator is a stage-by-stage map (pre-construction, construction, operation) showing what the guarantee does and does not cover in each phase, grounded in the official KPBU portal definition. Most SERP results conflate guarantee with subsidy or collateral; none explicitly partition coverage by stage. PT PII's credibility is direct: it is the issuer of these guarantees, making it the institution that draws this very line.
