# Article notes

## Angle summary
In project prep meetings, three people can say "dukungan pemerintah" and mean three entirely different things: one-time cash to cover a cost overrun (VGF), monthly availability payments, or a promise to pay compensation if the government breaches its contract (guarantee). Only one of those is actually a guarantee; asking for the wrong one means losing a budget cycle. The competitive gap is precise: the JDIH Kementerian Keuangan homepage has two different definitions for "Jaminan Pemerintah" on the same first-SERP page; Perpres 103/2020 (guarantee for loan defaults and bonds) ranks on the same keyword as a third meaning; and the search "perbedaan dukungan pemerintah dan jaminan pemerintah" returns only pages defining VGF alone, never a comparison table. The differentiator is a side-by-side taxonomy: who disburses it, when, under what condition, and how it affects APBN liability. PMK 68/2024 consolidated these three into a single regulation, making this the first moment to explain their differentiation clearly.
