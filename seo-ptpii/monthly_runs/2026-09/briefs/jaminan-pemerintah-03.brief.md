# Jaminan Pemerintah untuk KPBU Daerah: Jalur dan Batasnya

*(Meta title, 56 chars — primary keyword in first 18 chars, KPBU token at char 25. Meta description target 140–160 chars: "Proyek KPBU daerah bisa memperoleh jaminan pemerintah. Pahami siapa PJPK-nya, kriteria kelayakannya, dan konsekuensi fiskal bagi APBD." — 143 chars.)*

## Hook hypothesis

A kabupaten has a project that works on paper — a penerangan jalan umum scheme, a waste facility, a water system — and a bupati willing to sign. The team then hits a belief that circulates widely in daerah planning rooms: government guarantees are for national projects, for jalan tol and satellites, not for us. So the project never gets submitted, and the APBD absorbs a burden it did not have to carry alone.

The belief is wrong, and PT PII's own hub says so plainly: the guarantee covers government risk from the penanggung jawab proyek "baik pusat dan daerah". There are guaranteed daerah projects with signature dates on the record — Kabupaten Madiun's penerangan jalan umum scheme, the first KPBU APJ project in Indonesia, signed its Perjanjian Penjaminan on 20 September 2022 and reached commercial operation on 18 Juli 2023; Kabupaten Lombok Barat followed on 12 Oktober 2023.

What is genuinely different for a daerah project is not eligibility but **structure**: who holds the PJPK role, how a multi-year commitment sits against an annual APBD, and what obligations attach to the daerah when the guarantee is issued. That is the article — the access route and its fiscal consequences, written for a Bappeda or OPD team that needs to know whether to start.

PT PII is credible here as the single window through which every guarantee request arrives, and as the institution that signed those daerah agreements.

## Article skeleton — H2s the writer should expand

Every `## H2` is followed immediately by a 40–60-word direct answer before any elaboration (GEO §2a).

1. **H2 (definitional)**: `Apa itu jaminan pemerintah untuk proyek KPBU daerah?`
   *Covers*: the canonical definition, scoped to the daerah case, with the hub's own line that the guarantee covers government risk from the penanggung jawab proyek at both pusat and daerah level. Introduce **KPDBU** as the regional variant term now in use. **Mandatory disambiguation paragraph here**: not jaminan sosial/BPJS, not agunan or jaminan kebendaan, not jaminan pelaksanaan or jaminan penawaran in pengadaan barang dan jasa. Keep the infrastruktur/KPBU token in the opening sentence.

2. **H2 (question-format)**: `Siapa yang menjadi PJPK dalam proyek KPBU daerah?`
   *Covers*: the PJPK role can be held by a Menteri, Kepala Lembaga, **Kepala Daerah**, BUMN or BUMD. Explain what holding that role actually means — the guarantee is issued against *that* party's contractual obligations, so the identity of the PJPK determines whose budget the obligation ultimately tracks to. This H2 sets up section 4.

3. **H2 (practical/structured — NUMBERED LIST)**: `Lima kriteria kelayakan yang menentukan bisa atau tidaknya proyek dijamin`
   *Covers*: (1) harus merupakan Proyek KPBU sebagaimana diatur dalam **Perpres 38/2015**; (2) memenuhi ketentuan peraturan sektor terkait dengan pengadaan melalui lelang yang transparan dan kompetitif; (3) layak secara teknis, ekonomi, keuangan dan lingkungan serta tidak berdampak negatif secara sosial; (4) prastudi kelayakan dikerjakan konsultan independen dan profesional; (5) perjanjian KPBU memuat ketentuan arbitrase yang mengikat. For each, one line on what a daerah team must actually produce. Then the route: the request goes through a single window to PT PII as Badan Usaha Penjaminan Infrastruktur.
   ⚠️ The portal prints "Perpres 38/2005" — a typo. Write **Perpres 38/2015**.

4. **H2 (honest / counter-conventional)**: `Apa yang ditanggung daerah setelah jaminan terbit`
   *Covers*: the trust-builder, and the section that makes this article original. A guarantee is not free cover — it shifts timing and certainty of payment, not ultimate responsibility, and the obligations it creates attach to the daerah as PJPK across a multi-year horizon that an annual APBD cycle does not naturally accommodate.
   ⚠️ **Conditional content — researcher gate.** The Kemenkeu KPBU portal states that the regres mechanism does not apply where a Kementerian/Lembaga is the PJPK, which by implication means a daerah PJPK does carry a regres obligation. **This is MEDIUM confidence** — the page carrying it also cites revoked regulations. Either way, the section must not become a regres explainer.
   **Hard boundary: never assert or imply that a guarantee claim has ever been paid.** Discuss obligations and structure, never events.

5. **H2 (practical — TABLE REQUIRED)**: `Proyek daerah yang sudah dijamin: apa yang bisa dipelajari`
