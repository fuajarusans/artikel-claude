# Jaminan Pemerintah KPBU: Di Mana Tercatat dalam APBN?

*(Meta title, 52 chars — primary keyword in first 19 chars, KPBU token present. Meta description target 140–160 chars: "Jaminan pemerintah pada proyek KPBU tidak muncul di neraca APBN. Pelajari di mana kewajiban kontinjensi diungkapkan dan bagaimana eksposurnya dikelola." — 150 chars.)*

## Hook hypothesis

A PJPK finance team asks the question that sounds trivial and is not: if the government has guaranteed our project, where is that promise written down? Staff go looking for it in the neraca APBN and do not find it — and conclude either that the guarantee is not real, or that it is a hidden liability nobody is tracking. Both readings are wrong, and both damage the conversation with DPRD, with auditors, and with lenders.

The honest answer is a distinction most readers have never been taught: a guarantee that has not been called is **disclosed**, not **recognised**. It lives in the Catatan atas Laporan Keuangan of the LKPP and in the fiscal-risk chapter of the Nota Keuangan, not as a line of debt on the balance sheet. That is not an accounting loophole; it is the whole design, and it comes with four named control levers plus a capacity trigger that pulls the Kementerian Keuangan in alongside the guarantor.

Nothing on the live SERP explains this. The DJKN article that comes closest is from 23 Agustus 2016 and stops at defining direct versus contingent liability. KSAP's fiscal-risk page explicitly tells practitioners to go read SAP for the recognition and disclosure rules, and then does not cover them. PT PII can speak here credibly because ring-fencing the government's contingent liabilities is one of the stated reasons it was created — this is its own architecture, described from the inside.
