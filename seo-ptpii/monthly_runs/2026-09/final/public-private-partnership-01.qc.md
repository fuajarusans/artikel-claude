## What I fixed (changelog)
- **OECD local-costs bracket, internal contradiction + live discrepancy.** Draft read "40 percent … for high-income countries and 50 percent for lower-income countries … Which bracket applies is **ECA-specific**." The determinant is the *buyer country's* classification, not the ECA, so the sentence contradicted the clause before it. Rewrote to "Which bracket applies turns on the buyer country's classification under the Arrangement, and this review did not confirm Indonesia's" — which also carries the dossier's explicit flag forward. Separately, my web check surfaced the source framing as "high-income OECD countries / all other nations" rather than "lower-income countries", so I relabelled the second bracket to the neutral "for the rest", which is correct under either framing. See open flag.
- **Removed any possible inference of guarantee payout history.** "any claim paid carries *hak regres*" → "it carries *hak regres*, a right of recovery against the party responsible for any claim that is paid" (unambiguously conditional).
- **Portfolio scope precision.** "covering 61 projects worth more than IDR 732 trillion" → "covering 61 guaranteed projects, KPBU and non-KPBU, worth more than IDR 732 trillion", since the 61 is 38 KPBU + 23 non-KPBU (dossier) and the preceding clause is about the KPBU mandate. Reference date "as at June 2026" and the project-value / guarantee-value split were already correct and untouched.
- **Entity glosses for an international audience.** "ICIEC" → "ICIEC, part of the Islamic Development Bank Group"; "Badan Penyiapan" → "Badan Penyiapan (the preparation agency)".
- **Conclusion clarity + keyword.** Dangling pronoun "It moves faster when…" → "That assembly moves faster when…"; "Indonesia's PPP framework" → "Indonesia's public private partnership framework" (puts the exact primary keyword in the closing section, which the playbook §3 requires and the draft missed).
- **LSI gap closed, twice, at zero cost to the GEO band.** "carries a mismatch" → "carries a currency mismatch" (H2 #5 answer: 45w → 46w, still in band) and table cell "Narrows the mismatch" → "Narrows the currency mismatch". `currency mismatch project finance` is a declared secondary keyword and the exact phrase was absent.
